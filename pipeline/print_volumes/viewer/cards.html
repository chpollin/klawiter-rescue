Help and keys

Every value on a card was read from the print volumes by a rule and is bound to its passage in the entry text. Select a value and mark it right, wrong or unsure. Wrong asks for the right passage, typed or selected in the entry text, or for the mark that the value should not exist. A card is complete when every value it shows carries a decision.

The review sample holds a frozen set of objects, drawn by volume and part from entries that rule development has not seen. Only its complete cards enter the measurement. Decisions on other cards are kept as development reviews.

Wiki candidates come from the concordance and are proposals that nobody has confirmed. Every card leads to its page in the print volume, every wiki page to its permalink. The view is local, because the rights to the print volumes are unresolved.
