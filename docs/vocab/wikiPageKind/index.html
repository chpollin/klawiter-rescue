Klawiter Bibliography Vocabulary

klawiter:wikiPageKind

Property — wiki page kind

Kind of the source wiki page, read by rule: work-hub (the compiler's page of a work with its editions and translations), edition-page (a page the compiler files as one item in one language, with its publications), author-page (a page indexed under a person, holding items by or about that person), other (adaptation, event, periodical, cross-reference or index page) or undetermined (no rule decides). It describes the page, not a work identity.
